How it works
Three steps to eating right.
1

Scan your ingredients

Open the camera tab and point it at your fridge, pantry shelf, or groceries. The AI identifies everything in seconds. Or just type what you have.

2

Get AI-matched recipes

Plately generates 5 custom recipes from exactly what you've got. Every recipe shows full macros — calories, protein, carbs, fat — and cost in pesos per serving.

3

Cook + hit your macros

Follow the step-by-step guide with built-in timers. Tap "Finish Cooking" to log your macros instantly. Track your streak. Share your W.
