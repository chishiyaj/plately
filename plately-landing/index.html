Why Plately
Your fitness apps have it backwards.
MyFitnessPal, Cronometer — they log what you already ate. Plately flips it: plan before you cook, not after.

Pre-cook macro awareness

See your calories, protein, carbs, and fat before you even turn on the stove. Plan your meal, cook it, hit your targets — in that order.

How it works
Three steps to eating right.
1

Scan or type ingredients

Open the camera and point it at your fridge or pantry. The AI identifies everything instantly. Or type what you have — comma separated, no fuss.

2

Get AI-matched recipes

Plately generates 5 custom recipes from your exact ingredients. Every recipe shows full macros — calories, protein, carbs, fat — and cost in pesos per serving, before you cook.

3

Cook, log, and track

Step-by-step cooking guide with built-in timers. Tap "Finish Cooking" to log your macros instantly. Build a streak. Share your progress.
